    Reading 1 of 'spin about the axis' (E-ring + B-ring, m = 0): the azimuthal E×B circulation is there, but it integrates
    to J_z = 0 (the field is unchanged by a rotation about the axis); the handedness (χ, h) starts with the sign of the
    relative phase and then swings through both signs as the two modes (different frequencies) beat: it is not conserved,
    as expected where the dielectric breaks duality symmetry (Fernandez-Corbaton et al., PRL 111, 060401, 2013).
    Reading 2 (a pattern that turns, m = 1): net J_z, its sign set by the phase (the ± runs are mirror images: Minkowski
    +1.8898 / -1.8898, Abraham +0.0112 / -0.0112 at the start); its size is not a clean 1 on this grid (above), the Abraham form
    is small because the field's momentum sits in the dielectric, and Q is the standing mode's (1382 against 1383).
    ½ per quantum: J_z·ω/U is an energy-weighted mean of whole m, so a value of ½ can only be a mixture of states
    (the ½ E-ring + ½ m = 1 run), never one coherent classical mode.
    Measured: neutrinos are left-handed (helicity −½), antineutrinos right-handed. Maxwell's equations are parity-even, so the
    sign of J_z or h is set by how the field is started; nothing in the model fixes it. Standard prediction: a field's
    helicity and angular momentum are ordinary electromagnetism; neutrino handedness comes from the V−A weak interaction.
