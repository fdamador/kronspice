inverted after one trip and upright after two — the field returns to itself only after a 720° double loop.

Part 4 — three electron pumps that work (pumps.go)
   The model's own frequency, m_e c²/h = 1.24e+20 Hz, is about 10⁸ times beyond any circuit,
   so these pumps run at frequencies circuits reach. Electrons come from metal or ground; energy from a declared source.

   1. Single-electron pump (Pothier et al. 1992; orthodox tunnelling theory)
      3 junctions of 200 aF, 200 kΩ; gates 50 aF at 90°; 20 mK; charging energy e²/2C = 0.178 meV (2.1 K)
           drive    electrons per cycle          current  heat per electron (gates)         ledger
            1 MHz            1.000000000        0.1602 pA                  12.7 µeV       -4.1e-10
           10 MHz            0.999999977         1.602 pA                  53.1 µeV       -4.1e-08
           30 MHz            0.999458493         4.804 pA                  93.2 µeV       -3.7e-07
          100 MHz            0.950531648         15.23 pA                   160 µeV       -4.1e-06
          300 MHz            0.667354712         32.08 pA                   204 µeV       -1.0e-05
      uphill against 20 µV at 10 MHz: 0.999998 electrons per cycle; the gates supply 72.8 µeV per cycle =
      20.0 µeV lifted into the bias source + 52.8 µeV heat (ledger -5.6e-08)
      Exactly one electron per cycle at low drive (I = e·f); errors grow when the drive outruns the tunnelling (RC ≈ 40 ps).

   2. Multipactor pump in an RF resonator (Farnsworth US 2,091,439; Kishek & Lau 1998)
      1 mm copper gap, 1 cm², resonator 1 GHz (C = 0.885 pF, L = 28.6 nH, Q = 1000); drive 0.779 mA for 140 V unloaded
      resonator alone, own integrator vs kronspice transient: largest difference 0.0031 of V0
      in step (140 V, seed 1000 electrons): grows to 1.98e+08 electrons in the gap, then pulls the gap voltage down to 39 V,
      stops multiplying; the resonator recovers and a stray electron (one per 20 ns while the gap is empty, declared) restarts it:
      bursts, as Kishek & Lau found (7 strays used)
      out of step (300 V): 0 electrons left after 40 periods — no pumping.
      over 2000 periods (2000 ns): 2.16e+08 electrons collected, average collector current 15.2 µA (second 1000 ns)
      ledger [nJ]: drive 49.32 = resonator heat 22.98 + impacts on the plates 31.43 + collector 1.78 + stored change -6.876; residual -3.5e-07
      (the stored change is negative: the bursts also drain the energy the resonator held at the start)
      energy per collected electron: 1426 eV from the RF drive

   3. Ground charge pump with a resonant drive (§3j; Cockcroft & Walton 1932)
      10 V at 100 kHz into a series resonator (Q = 20) → 3-stage negative ladder → 100 nF bank, 10 MΩ load; 600 cycles
      resonator step-up: 10 V → 199.9 V peak (×20.0); bank -1194.7 V against the ideal −2·n·peak = -1199.5 V
      electrons on the bank's negative plate 7.46e+14 (plus 4.02e+12 through the load), lent by the earth; the bank's earthed plate
      returns the same number, so the earth's net charge is unchanged.
      ledger: source 3.559 J = heat 3.425 J + stored 0.133 J (bank 0.0714 J) + time-stepping error 2.7e-05 J

Part 5 — the multipactor pump driven by a standard microwave-oven magnetron, evolved (magnetron.go)
   source: LG 2M214 (oven magnetron), 2450 MHz, 900 W RF; declared efficiency 70 % + 34.6 W heater → 1320 W from the mains
   chain: magnetron → circulator → coupling port → copper re-entrant cavity (Q0 = 2000) whose gap multipacts → grid → collector;
   power the cavity does not take goes to the circulator's water load. Genes: gap, gap area (side ≤ λ/10), coupling β,
   grid transparency, surface. Fitness: mains watts per collected µA. Limits: Kilpatrick field, gap ≤ 10 kV (non-relativistic).

Part 6 — scaling up: many multipactor gaps sharing one magnetron (magnetron.go, gene "gaps")
   N identical gaps take Pout/N each through an ideal isolated power divider (declared lossless); the genetic
   algorithm now also picks N (1 to 1,000,000). Electrons multiply in proportion to the copper area in the field, so
   current scales with total gap area and with microwave power; the fitness is still mains watts per collected µA.
