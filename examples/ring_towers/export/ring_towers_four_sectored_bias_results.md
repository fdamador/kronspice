## Kron's diakoptic solution

XII §4) into **4 pieces** of [75 75 75 75] unknowns each (node voltages, plus the branch currents of voltage sources and inductors), joined by **4 links** (the torn branches). Each piece is factored on its own; the only coupled solve is the interconnection system of order **0**, against 300 unknowns for the whole circuit.
