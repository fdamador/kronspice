1. The force-free field (Nachamkin eq. 8, l = 1; the spheromak field)
   ∇×F = αF to 2e-10, ∇·F = 0 to 1e-11; a traced field line keeps its flux ψ to 1e-14 (it stays on its torus)
   the tori nest around a current ring at 0.611 of the radius (61.1 mm for R = 10 cm): the plasmoid's "electron ring"

2. His critical frequency is the cold-plasma cutoff; Appendix B recomputed (R = 10 cm, k = 2686 /m)
   charge density ρ = (m/e)ε0c²(k² − α²) = -32.63 C/m³ (his −32.63), 8.532e+17 electrons (his 8.532×10¹⁷)
   that density's plasma frequency satisfies ω² = ωp² + α²c² to -4e-17: his plasmoid is a standing wave of a cold
   plasma whose wavenumber is α, and his critical frequency ω0 = αc is the cutoff of that wave.
   his field for C = 0.01 at 126 GHz: E0 = C·R·ω²/|e/m| = 3.566e+09 V/m (his 3.566×10⁹)
   but then the electrons' oscillation speed is ω·C·R = 7.92e+08 m/s = 2.64 c — above the speed of light, so his
   non-relativistic electron model does not hold for his own example; real electrons would be relativistic and
   the field ~1,000 times air's breakdown strength.

3. Does the resonant size stop radiation? l = 1 resonances of the plasma sphere (exact, outgoing waves only)
   TE (his M part)  128.1583 GHz, needs αR = 4.4933; Q = 4.8e+05; energy lifetime 6e-07 s
   TM (his N part)  128.1471 GHz, needs αR = 2.7437; Q = 9.4e+05; energy lifetime 1.2e-06 s
   The plasmoid radiates: with nothing feeding it from outside, its energy leaks away in about a microsecond.
   And the two halves of the force-free field need different sizes (αR = 4.49 and 2.74): an equal mix of them,
   which is what makes E parallel to H, is not one free mode of the sphere. It would need driving or surface
   currents, the weak point Nachamkin names himself. At 1 MJ the initial radiated power is ~2e+12 W.

   reported to last seconds cannot be a resistive spheromak in air.

5. A collapsing electron ring (electron-ring compressor; bank and coil in kronspice)
   bank 1000 µF at 10 kV → coil 10 µH (16 turns/m): field 0.050 → 1.984 T in a quarter period
   ring radius 16.4 → 2.60 cm (law R ∝ B^-½: 2.60 cm); momentum ×6.3007 (law ×6.2989)
   electron energy 2.0 → 15.0 MeV; work of the induced field 2.0808e-12 J = energy gain 2.0808e-12 J per electron
   canonical angular momentum (cyclic azimuth) held to 6e-04; ring current with 10¹³ electrons 456 → 2933 A
   the bank (5e+04 J) drives the coil; the ring takes 21 J of it; kronspice ledger -1e-09
   The ring collapses because the field around it rises, and every joule it gains comes from the bank.

No energy appears from the plasmoid or the void inside the ring: radiation, resistance and the bank account for all of it.
