## Kron's diakoptic solution

The circuit is torn automatically (ground is the common bus of all pieces, Happ Ch. XII §4) into **2 pieces** of [6 3] unknowns each (node voltages, plus the branch currents of voltage sources and inductors), joined by **1 links** (the torn branches). Each piece is factored on its own; the only coupled solve is the interconnection system of order **1**, against 9 unknowns for the whole circuit.
