Part 6. Why no shell reaches g = 2
  g = Mc²/E_L = 1 + E_ether/E_L. Scaling the void by λ, the light's energy goes as 1/λ, the ether's
  pressure work as λ³ and its wall as λ². At balance E_L = 3E_P + 2E_W, so
  g = 1 + (E_P + E_W)/(3E_P + 2E_W): between 4/3 (pressure) and 3/2 (wall). The round bubbles lie there; the
  excited modes dip just below 4/3 (1.31–1.32) because the ether's opacity is finite and the light leaks into
  the wall, so its energy is not purely 1/λ. Every state computed here has g = 1.31–1.37, about 32–35 % below 2.
  g = 2 needs an ether energy that grows as λ¹ — a constant-force string. In this ether only a vortex
  line does that, and ether_horizon Part 13 found vortex rings travel or unwind. Shells only add walls
  (λ²) and layers (λ³), so they cannot move g toward 2; the light squeezes them out anyway.
