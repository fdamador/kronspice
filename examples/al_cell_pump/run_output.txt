H38b — one tank per rectifier: concentric pipes
  rod at the centre, pipes around it, the tank wall outside; each annulus of water is one cell.
  parallel: pipes alternate aluminium and iron, all aluminium on one bus bar and all iron on the other —
            every annulus is the same valve between the buses; ends need no seals (neighbouring annuli
            sit at the same voltage); the tank holds one forming voltage
  series:   clad pipes (aluminium outside, iron inside) carry the current across every annulus in turn;
            the tank holds N forming voltages, but each pipe's ends must be sealed (water around an end
            shorts a cell); evolved with perfect seals, then swept. Plain, the inner annulus (least
            aluminium, least film) takes most of the reverse voltage; graded, the outer pipes' aluminium
            is masked to give every annulus the same area, and they share it equally
