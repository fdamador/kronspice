ether_knot (H29 step 2): does a nonlinear ether hold a knot of field energy at a fixed size?
Energy E = κ2∫(∂n)² + κ4∫|B_n|²: the ether's stiffness plus the knotted light's magnetic energy
(B_n = −16 × Rañada's knotted B for the starting map). Units: κ2 = κ4 = 1, length ℓ = √(κ4/κ2).
Axisymmetric (Q = 1) relaxation from Rañada's Hopf map; arrested Newton flow, dt = 0.05 h.

   linking of preimage curves (field lines of B_n): 1.0001 (gap 0.019 ℓ), 1.0002 (gap 0.011 ℓ) — Hopf charge 1

Standard prediction: Derrick (1964) — the magnetic term alone spreads the knot, the stiffness alone
shrinks it; together they fix its size at ℓ = √(κ4/κ2) and its energy, ≈ 548·√(κ2κ4) by this run (Faddeev & Niemi 1997).
What the model does not fix: κ2 and κ4 themselves — no measured ether stiffness exists to set them.
