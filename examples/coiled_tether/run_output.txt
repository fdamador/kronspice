   straight 5-mile tether        1858.792 V
   coiled (ordinary)                0.888 V   = E' × the coil's 3.84 m length: the turns cancel
   coiled (Tesla bifilar)           0.889 V   same: both strands run the same way through the field
   straight bifilar (out/back)   1.14e-13 V   out and back cancel
   field gradient across the orbit adds at most N·A·v·3B/r ≈ 0.00041 V (a dipole field)
   All of it is quasi-DC: the field seen by the craft changes over an orbit (~90 min), far below any LC resonance.
   coil spinning at 1 rev/s in the field: AC EMF 0.759 V peak at 1 Hz (energy from the spin, which slows)
