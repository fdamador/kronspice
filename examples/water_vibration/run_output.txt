H2O is fitted (0 % by construction); D2O, HDO and H2-18O are predictions with the same bonds.
The remaining error is anharmonicity. A measured fundamental sits below the harmonic frequency by
a shift that shrinks with heavier atoms (ω·x ∝ 1/μ against ω ∝ 1/√μ). A harmonic force field fitted
to H2O's fundamentals absorbs H's larger shift, so it comes out soft and under-predicts deuterium
modes by about 1 %; the O–H stretch of HDO, closest to H2O, is within 0.1 %.
