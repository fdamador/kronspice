# 9. The electron test bench

*Planned.* Classical models of the electron as rings, vortices and
circulating light, each solved by the engine and measured against the
same bench: charge, magnetic moment, g-factor, Zeeman and resonance
behaviour, with the Dirac and QED values beside every result
(doc/kelvin-references.md, doc/tewari-references.md; H13, H21, H23).

Examples: [kelvin_ring](../examples/kelvin_ring),
[vortex_ring](../examples/vortex_ring),
[space_vortex](../examples/space_vortex),
[kron_electron](../examples/kron_electron),
[photon_electron](../examples/photon_electron).

Planned sections: Kelvin's 1899 charged ring and his own Zeeman test;
vortex atoms through the ring-electromagnet analogy; Tewari's
space-vortex electron and its g = 15/8; a ring of charge as one of Kron's
machines, with g ≤ 1 and the resonance frequencies that discriminate it;
the toroidal photon, and what the field solver says about its
confinement.
