# 3. Kron's machines

*Planned.* Gabriel Kron's view of a circuit as a geometric object
(CLAUDE.md §3a): the primitive network, the connection tensor, change of
reference frame, and the machine element whose inductances depend on a
rotor angle, with speed voltages and torque from Christoffel symbols
(§3l, curvature 3).

Examples: [kron_machine](../examples/kron_machine),
[parametric_pump](../examples/parametric_pump),
[parametric_lcr](../examples/parametric_lcr),
[homopolar](../examples/homopolar),
[homopolar_bearing](../examples/homopolar_bearing),
[bearing_motor](../examples/bearing_motor),
[kron_nmr](../examples/kron_nmr).

Planned sections: frames I and II and the induction motor starting up;
a parametric inductance against Mathieu, with the pump's work on the
ledger; Tesla's homopolar dynamo as a machine element; load current
through a ball bearing and what the measurements allow; Bloch's
equations as a three-winding machine in a rotating frame.
