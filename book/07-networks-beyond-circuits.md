# 7. Networks beyond circuits

*Planned.* Kron's and Branin's network analogies (CLAUDE.md §3a.1 item
3): every channel has an across and a through variable whose product is
power, so mechanics, acoustics, molecular vibration and the Schrödinger
equation become networks the same engine solves.

Examples: [acoustic_drive](../examples/acoustic_drive),
[water_vibration](../examples/water_vibration),
[dna_vibration](../examples/dna_vibration),
[kron_schrodinger](../examples/kron_schrodinger).

Planned sections: generator to piezo ring to a liquid sphere's breathing
mode, with every watt placed; atoms as mass capacitors and bonds as
spring inductors, fitted to H₂O and predicting D₂O; the four DNA bases
and the Watson–Crick pairs, hydrogen bonds as their own group, stacks and
sound; Kron's 1945 Schrödinger network and the H₂⁺ bond.
