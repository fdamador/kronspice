# 5. Fields from Maxwell

*Planned.* The `em/` package (CLAUDE.md §3c): Maxwell's equations in
Hamilton's quaternion form and their triple-twin checks, lumped
parameters extracted from field solves, the quasi-static validity check,
the Yee field solver with its exact discrete energy ledger, and
rate-dependent materials.

Examples: [pcb_layout](../examples/pcb_layout),
[rotating_efield](../examples/rotating_efield),
[rate_of_change](../examples/rate_of_change).

Planned sections: a microstrip pair's L, C and K from a two-dimensional
field solve, evolved for 50 Ω and low crosstalk; displacement current,
Rowland's disk and the Röntgen–Eichenwald mirror of the homopolar
machine; water's Debye response as a circuit twin and where higher time
derivatives enter the field equations (H19).
