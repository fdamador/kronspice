## Negative resistance and nonlinear elements (added during M5)

- **Negative resistors** (`R1 a b -500`, as in ngspice). The power they
  deliver, v²/|R|, is **model-supplied**: reported per element in every
  ledger (`.op`, `.ac`, `.tran`), balancing the books, never counted as
  inflow from the exterior. Tested: divider with a negative leg, AC gain of
  2, an LC tank whose oscillation grows as e^(t/2|R|C) with the stored-energy
  gain equal to the model-supplied energy, and exact cancellation (R ∥ −R)
  reported as a singular matrix.
- **Nonlinear elements in both forms** (`device/behavioral.go`, ngspice B
  source with `pwl`):
  - voltage-controlled, i = f(v) (N-type, tunnel diode):
    `B1 a b I=pwl(V(a,b), v1,i1, …)`, `Builder.NonlinearVC`;
  - current-controlled, v = g(i) (S-type, arc or glow lamp):
    `B1 m b V=pwl(I(Vs), i1,v1, …)` with a 0 V sensing source in series,
    `Builder.NonlinearCC`;
  - the other two combinations are controlled sources (all of their power
    model-supplied).
  Negative-slope regions are allowed; curves through the 2nd/4th quadrant
  supply power, which is model-supplied.
- **Several operating points.** `Options.NodeSet` (SPICE `.nodeset`) picks
  among them: the tunnel-diode test reaches each of its three exact
  solutions from a matching guess.
- **Pseudo-transient continuation** completes the DC fallback chain. It is
  the only step that solves a glow lamp driven above its striking voltage
  (Newton, gmin and source stepping all stall at the turning point).
- **Open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current** of any terminal pair
  (`analysis.OpenShort`), with the full nonlinear solve; GA objectives
  `port_voc` and `port_isc`.

## The engine (`evo/`)

- **Genes**: element value or `.model` parameter; log scale between min and
  max; optional E6/E12/E24/E48/E96 snapping (IEC 60063); sign `positive`,
  `negative` or `either`.
- **Operators**: tournament selection, BLX-0.5 crossover on log magnitudes,
  uniform crossover of signs, Gaussian mutation in decades, sign flips,
  elitism.
- **Objectives** (weighted sum, each a non-negative error): `cutoff`,
  `gain_at`, `op_voltage`, `tran_mean`, `tran_ripple`, `port_voc`,
  `port_isc`, `model_supplied` (limit). Each analysis runs once per
  candidate and is shared by the objectives that use it. Every individual
  carries the model-supplied power seen while scoring it.
- **Outputs** (`runs/<timestamp>-<name>/`): `config.json`, `stats.csv` (best,
  mean, worst, diversity in decades, failures, evaluations, best gene
  values), `best/genNNNN.cir`, `best.cir`, `checkpoint.json` (written
  atomically after every generation). Netlists are plain SPICE.
- **CLI**: `go run ./cmd/evolve -config examples/rc_lowpass_values/config.json`
  (`-resume <run dir>`, `-workers`, `-out`, `-quiet`; Ctrl-C stops cleanly
  with the checkpoint written).

## Other changes

- `circuit.WriteNetlist` and `Circuit.Clone`; round-trip tested.
- Transient ledger: each point's residual is judged against the whole run's
  peak power after the run (a sine drive just after t = 0 made all powers
  ~1e-14 W while round-off was set by the run's scale); gross imbalances
  (> 1e-3) still stop a run immediately.
- Diode stored energy in closed form (was numerical), exact in every region.
