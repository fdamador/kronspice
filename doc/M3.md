## What was added

- **`solver.CDense`** — complex LU with partial pivoting, allocation-free solve.
- **AC stamps on every device** (`LoadAC`, `OperatingAC`): conductance,
  jωC, inductor branch V − jωL·I = 0, AC source phasors (`AC mag phase`).
- **`.ac dec|oct|lin points fstart fstop`** — operating point first, then
  one complex solve per frequency (SPICE3 sweep spacing).
- **AC power ledger** per frequency: average power ½·Re(V·I*) from AC
  sources equals resistor dissipation; reactive power of capacitors and
  inductors reported; complex Tellegen residual checked (≤ 1e-9).
- **`.dc src start stop step [src2 start2 stop2 step2]`** — one or two
  nested source sweeps, ledger checked at every point, the source's own value
  restored afterwards.
- **Measurements** (`analysis/measure.go`): `Transfer`, `GainAt`,
  `Cutoff3dB`, `Crossing`, `RiseTime`, `Overshoot`, `SettlingTime`. Undefined
  measurements return `ErrNoMeasurement` so the GA can penalize them.
- **Output:** `output.ACResult`, `output.DCSweepResult`, CSV writers (AC:
  `vm(node)`, `vp(node)` in degrees), text summaries.
- **CLI:** runs `.op`, `.dc`, `.ac` and `.tran` in netlist order; `-o` writes
  the last one as CSV.

## Change to the transient ledger check

The per-point residual is now divided by the **largest Σ|power| seen so far
in the run** instead of the instantaneous Σ|power|. When every power in a
ringing circuit passes through zero at the same instant, round-off
(≈1e-16 W) was being compared with momentarily tiny powers and flagged as an
imbalance. Round-off scales with the largest quantities in the solve, so the
run's peak is the right yardstick. A real imbalance still fails.
