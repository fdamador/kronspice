## What was built

**Orthogonal frame (`kron/orthogonal.go`).** The closed paths are the
fundamental loops C; the open paths P are the junction pairs from each
junction to its sub-network's datum. `[C │ P]` spans the branch space (Kron
1939 p. 75). `Network.Invariants` computes Kron's two power invariants of a
solution — the input `e·i` of the impressed emfs on the closed paths and the
output `E·I` of the impressed currents on the open paths (Kron 1939 p. 409,
pp. 412–413) — in the primitive frame and again from orthogonal
coordinates; they agree to round-off on every twin circuit, and
`e·i − E·I` equals the ledger's net exterior input.

**Exterior (`kron/exterior.go`).** Every element has one open path per
channel to that channel's exterior junction Ω (`Exterior.Junction`). The
power through an active path comes from the element's own port model
(`device.ExteriorPower`, the model the MNA ledger uses), applied to the
voltage and current of its branch in Kron's frame; an inactive path is
present and carries exactly zero (Kron 1939 p. 485). The ledger is built
from the open paths by `em.BuildLedger`, so it is the same kind of object
as the MNA one and the two are compared row by row.

**Chords (`kron/chords.go`).** `Network.AddChord(a, b, z(ω), reciprocal)`
adds a TAN interaction term: element a's voltage gains `z(ω)·i_b` (Durand,
Maurice, Reineix, WCE 2013; Maurice et al., arXiv:1412.0187). Mutual
inductance is `z = jωM`; a coupling with a propagation delay is
`z·e^(−jωτ)`, TAN's affine source term in the frequency domain, checked
against the analytic two-loop answer. A chord on a branch whose current is
impressed (I, F, G) is refused: such a branch has no voltage equation.

**Radiation edges.** `Network.AddRadiation(element, R_rad(ω))` puts a
primitive branch of its own in series with the element, through a new
internal junction, so the element's heat and its radiated power stay
separate. `SmallLoopRadiation(area, turns)` gives Balanis's small-loop
resistance. Radiated power is booked on the light/radiation channel (the
§3.1 channel for emitted electromagnetic energy). Like inductors, a
radiation edge keeps its current as an axis in the node form: its tiny
resistance would otherwise turn into an admittance of 10⁵ S and lose
accuracy.

## Mutual inductance (K) in the whole engine

The chord test needs an MNA `K` to compare with, so K is now a full element:

- netlist `Kname L1 L2 k` (0 < k ≤ 1), `circuit.Mutual`, `Builder.Mutual`,
  netlist writer, validation (two distinct inductors, one K per pair);
- `device/mutual.go`: M = k·√(L1·L2); AC stamps −jωM between the two branch
  rows; a transient step integrates each inductor's total flux L1·i1 +
  M·i2 (backward Euler and trapezoidal); stores M·i1·i2; 0 allocations,
  10 ns per Load;
- golden circuits `mutual_ac` (a tuned 1:2 transformer, loop equations
  solved independently) and `mutual_tran` (step response, exact solution by
  matrix exponential), both backends;
- a lossless coupled pair keeps ½L1i1² + ½L2i2² + M·i1·i2 + ½Cv² constant
  over 2 ms (`TestMutualInductanceEnergy`), and the ledger closes.

## Not done here

- Non-electrical channel networks with across/through variables (thermal,
  mechanical, acoustic) joined at the exterior junctions: the open paths
  exist, but no element has such a coupling model yet.
- Tearing (M9b); the reflecting-environment radiation case (above).
